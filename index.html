About me

Curious about the dark corners of the internet, and wanting to make it safer.

I'm John Carl Alvaro, but everyone calls me JC. I'm a 4th-year IT student from Plaridel, Bulacan, and cybersecurity is the part of IT I enjoy most.

What got me into it is how much I enjoy digging into the parts of the internet most people never see. I want to help make it safer for the people who need it. I go to cybersecurity seminars, read up on how different types of viruses work, and risk management is my favorite topic in class.

I've built SariSync, a POS and inventory system, and Anatomia, a 3D anatomy learning app for my capstone. I'm also Vice Chairman of my parish's youth commission (SPCY), where I lead event planning and logistics, so I'm used to working with people.

I'm not chasing one specific role yet. I want to learn how the industry really works and meet people who've been in the field.
